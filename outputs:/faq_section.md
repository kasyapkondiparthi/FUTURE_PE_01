# Frequently Asked Questions

## How does QuizMaster Pro work?
Faculty create quizzes, students attempt them online, and results are generated instantly.

## Can students access quizzes from mobile devices?
Yes. The platform is fully responsive and works on phones, tablets, and computers.

## Is the platform suitable for coaching centers?
Yes. It is designed specifically for coaching institutes and educational organizations.

## Can faculty track student performance?
Yes. Detailed reports and analytics are available.

## Is technical knowledge required?
No. The interface is simple and easy to use.
